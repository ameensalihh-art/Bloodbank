KNOW YOUR BLOOD GROUP

Blood Group Compatibility

Understanding blood groups is important during blood donation and transfusion.

A+

A Positive

Can donate to A+ and AB+.

Can receive from A+, A-, O+, O-.

A-

A Negative

Can donate to A+, A-, AB+, AB-.

Can receive from A- and O-.

B+

B Positive

Can donate to B+ and AB+.

Can receive from B+, B-, O+, O-.

B-

B Negative

Can donate to B+, B-, AB+, AB-.

Can receive from B- and O-.

AB+

AB Positive

Can donate to AB+.

Can receive from all blood groups.

AB-

AB Negative

Can donate to AB+ and AB-.

Can receive from AB-, A-, B-, O-.

O+

O Positive

Can donate to O+, A+, B+, AB+.

Can receive from O+ and O-.

O-

O Negative

Can donate to all blood groups.

Can receive from O-.

ABOUT LIFEDROP

Connecting Donors With Those Who Need Help

LifeDrop is a blood donation center directory designed to help people easily find blood banks and donation centers in India.

Our goal is to make information about blood donation centers easier to access and encourage more people to become blood donors.
